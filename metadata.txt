about=EarthStream is the NASA Earthdata companion to SatStream. It searches NASA's Common Metadata Repository for the current map view or a polygon layer, lists matching granules in an Imagery Footprint table, and streams Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFFs straight into the map with an Earthdata Login token. Selected scenes can be clipped and downloaded, or aggregated into cloud-masked composites (median, mean, max, min, standard deviation, valid count, change between the first and last clear date) of spectral indices such as NDVI, EVI, NBR and MNDWI, surface-water frequency, disturbance, land surface temperature, elevation, slope and hillshade. A time-series table and chart summarise each date within the area. Everything runs on the standard library, GDAL and NumPy that ship with QGIS; nothing needs to be installed. A free Earthdata Login account is required for streaming and downloads.
